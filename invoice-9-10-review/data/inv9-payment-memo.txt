ʻEwa Development Plan (ʻEwa DP) Five-Year Review 
Alta Planning + Design, Inc. Invoice No. 304.RA24020.001-003 (Invoice #9, May 1–22, 2026)

Attached is Invoice #9, Invoice No. 304.RA24020.001-003, dated May 22, 2026, in the amount of $15,754.55 for professional services performed under the ʻEwa DP contract during the billing period of May 1 through May 22, 2026.

The invoice is accompanied by a consultant cover memorandum, project status report, and supporting documentation. During this billing period, work focused on continued development of the technical memoranda, project coordination, neighborhood profile preparation, and public engagement planning. The consultant coordinated with DPP and HoLIS to obtain residential pipeline project data and integrated updated growth projection information into the land use technical memorandum. Work also continued on the technical memoranda for land use, infrastructure, sustainability, and healthy communities, including coordination with subconsultants and review of relevant plans, policies, and background information.

The consultant continued regular project management activities, including coordination with subconsultants, bi-monthly communication with DPP staff, preparation of project updates, invoicing, and coordination regarding the proposed contract amendment. Additional work included reviewing and refining the public engagement strategy, continuing development of the project website and public contact intake form, updating neighborhood profile layouts based on DPP comments, compiling neighborhood-scale data and maps, and coordinating with HoLIS on network analysis mapping. The consultant also reviewed agency meeting notes and incorporated key takeaways into ongoing technical work.

The consultant provided several notes regarding this invoice in their project status report, which were subsequently discussed with DPP on July 14, 2026. During the meeting, DPP and the consultant agreed to more closely scrutinize future billings, place greater emphasis on deliverable-based billing rather than hourly expenditures, and exercise greater care in the use of project contingency funds.

Based on review of the invoice and supporting documentation, the billed amount is consistent with the scope of work and level of effort for this phase of the project.

The current contract expiration date is November 30, 2029. Should you have any questions, please contact me at (808) 768-8050 or by email at min.bu@honolulu.gov.
